# AlphaLab Roadmap

This document outlines the planned evolution of AlphaLab after the v1.0.0 release.

The roadmap is intended as a guide rather than a strict schedule.

Priorities may change based on community feedback and project needs.

# Long-Term Vision

AlphaLab aims to become a complete quantitative research platform covering

- Market Data
- Feature Engineering
- Research
- Portfolio Construction
- Machine Learning
- Production Trading
- Cloud Infrastructure
- Enterprise Deployment

while preserving its core engineering principles of

- Determinism
- Immutability
- Modular Architecture
- Event-Driven Design
- Production Readiness

---

# Versioning

Major releases introduce architectural milestones.

Minor releases introduce new capabilities.

Patch releases focus on stability, bug fixes, and performance improvements.
