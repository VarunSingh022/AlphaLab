# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open, respectful, and collaborative community.

AlphaLab welcomes contributors of all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We believe that a healthy community is built on mutual respect, constructive communication, and professional collaboration.

By participating in this project, you agree to help create an environment that is welcoming, inclusive, and focused on building high-quality open-source software.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive community include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Being open to different viewpoints and ideas.
- Accepting feedback gracefully.
- Focusing discussions on technical topics and project improvement.
- Helping new contributors learn the project.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or abusive language.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Spam or intentionally misleading contributions.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Community interactions related to AlphaLab

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately by opening a confidential issue or contacting the project maintainers.

All reports will be handled respectfully and reviewed in good faith.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these guidelines.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.
