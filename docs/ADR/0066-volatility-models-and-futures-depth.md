# ADR-0066: Volatility Models and Futures Depth -- Every Model a Stated Estimate, Every Roll an Identified Transform

## Status

**Accepted** (2026-11-23), under the owner's delegation of the programme's
decisions within the charter's principles (ADR-0056, 2026-11-10). D-V3 gave
each boundary reopening to the milestone that needs it, and this is M10's for
volatility (plan section G.2, engine N14, gaps GAP-25 and GAP-51, workstream
WS-28, scenarios S-03 and S-04).

It reverses one accepted boundary, narrowly: **BDY-015**'s "parametric fits
(SVI/SABR) stay out". It is reopened as models that are estimates, each fit
recording every choice it rests on. Exotics beyond Monte Carlo-priceable
vanillas and barriers, and structured products, stay out (plan section Y).

---

# Context

**What exists.**
- `options` prices with Black-Scholes-Merton and Cox-Ross-Rubinstein,
  inverts implied volatility, and holds surfaces as data, interpolated
  across expiries by name (FEA-005).
- `futures` has chains, roll rules and continuous series. The series are
  computed standalone, with no identity.

**BDY-015's reason.** A parametric fit is a model with parameters somebody
has to choose and defend, not a reading of quotes. That was the reason it
stayed out, and it is also how it comes in: since ADR-0058 an estimate is a
value with an identity that records its inputs, method and choices. A model
fitted that way is chosen and defended, in the record.

**What researchers cannot do (GAP-25).** Fit SVI or SABR to a smile, refuse a
surface with static arbitrage, read a local-volatility surface, price under
Heston, or price a path-dependent payoff by Monte Carlo. Nor can they run a
futures strategy whose continuous series is a dataset transform with its own
identity, where a pack's holiday moves a roll date (S-03).

# Decision

## 1. Volatility models are estimates

- **`options.models`** (N14) fits:
  - SVI, in its raw parameterization, per expiry;
  - SABR, by Hagan's expansion, per expiry, with beta stated;
  - Heston, across expiries.

  Each fit is an estimate (ADR-0058). Its record holds the quotes and their
  provenance, the objective and its weights, the bounds, the starting point,
  the method and tolerance, the parameters, and every quote's fit error.
- **Nothing is defaulted** (AC-12). SABR's beta, the weights, the bounds and
  the starting point are stated. A fit that does not converge within its
  tolerance returns its status and no parameters.
- **Calibration recovery is the test.** Parameters are recovered from
  synthetic smiles generated by the model itself, to a stated tolerance.

## 2. Static arbitrage is refused, by name

- **Butterfly**: each expiry's density is non-negative. For SVI this is
  Gatheral's condition, checked on a stated grid.
- **Calendar**: total variance does not decrease with maturity at any
  moneyness.
- A fitted surface that violates either is refused, naming the expiry and
  the region. A researcher who wants the violation measured rather than
  refused asks for the diagnostic explicitly.

## 3. Local volatility and Monte Carlo

- **Local volatility**, by Dupire's formula in total variance, is read only
  from a surface that passes section 2.
- **A Monte Carlo pricer** values European and barrier payoffs under stated
  dynamics: GBM, local volatility, or Heston with a stated discretization. It
  reports its standard error. Its seed is required (ADR-0029), and the same
  seed gives the same bits on every host.
- **Budgets.** An SVI fit per expiry within one second. Monte Carlo with
  1e5 paths within 30 seconds in pure Python, or through the compute
  backend's cross-check (M11).

## 4. Models mark, they do not book

- A position may be marked by a declared model as a diagnostic: a valuation
  recorded with the model's identity. The book of record is unchanged:
  positions trade and settle at market prices, as every position does.

## 5. Futures: a roll is an identified dataset transform

- **A continuous futures series is a dataset transform.** It states:
  - the chain and the roll rule, such as days before expiry, or a volume or
    open-interest switch;
  - the adjustment: none, difference or ratio;
  - the calendar its days are counted in.

  Its identity is a digest of all of that. A run that trades on it cites the
  identity in its provenance (S-03).
- **Calendars come from packs or the researcher** (ADR-0059). A pack's
  holiday moves a roll date, and a missing calendar entry is refused.

## 6. Non-FX forwards

- **`ForwardTerms`** declares:
  - the underlying;
  - the delivery date;
  - cash or physical settlement;
  - for a physical forward, the deliverable and its quantity.

  It rides on `InstrumentRecord` outside the identity key. `AssetType.FORWARD`
  is appended (GAP-51). A forward settles at its delivery date by its terms,
  and its value before then is a stated valuation.

# Compatibility

- Additive. Models, checks, a pricer, a dataset transform, forward terms and
  an appended `AssetType` member.
- No identity, digest or persisted byte moves for a run that declares none.
  The existing pricing and interpolation are unchanged.

# Known limitations

None is accepted at drafting. What implementation settles, or defers, is
recorded below.

# Consequences

- An options book can be researched from fitted, arbitrage-checked surfaces
  under scenario margin, with exercise and assignment as M5 delivered them
  (S-04).
- A global futures trend and carry strategy runs on identified continuous
  series whose roll dates follow its calendars (S-03).
- BDY-015 is narrowed in the ledger to what stays out: exotics beyond
  vanillas and barriers, and structured products.
